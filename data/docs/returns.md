# Returns and Exchanges

## Return window
You can return unused items with original tags within 30 days of delivery. Items must be clean and unworn outdoors. Footwear must be tried on indoors only.

## Final sale items
These cannot be returned: clearance items marked "Final Sale", opened fuel canisters, opened food and water-treatment products, and personalized or custom-embroidered gear.

## How to start a return
Go to the Returns page, enter your order number and email, choose the items and print the prepaid label. Drop the package at any UPS location.

## Return shipping cost
Exchanges ship free both ways. For refunds, a $7.50 return label fee is deducted from your refund. Returns of defective or wrong items are always free.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after our warehouse receives your return. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.

## Gifts
Gift returns are refunded as Pinecrest store credit to the person returning the item. The original buyer is not notified.
