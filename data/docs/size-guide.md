# Size Guide

## Jackets and tops (unisex chest measurement)
- XS: 32-34 inches
- S: 35-37 inches
- M: 38-40 inches
- L: 41-43 inches
- XL: 44-46 inches
- XXL: 47-49 inches

If you are between two sizes, choose the larger size for insulated jackets so you can layer underneath.

## Hiking boots and trail shoes
Pinecrest boots run about half a size small. We recommend ordering half a size up from your usual sneaker size. Try boots on in the afternoon with the socks you will hike in, because feet swell during the day. Boots come in regular and wide widths.

## Backpacks
Backpack size depends on torso length, not height. Measure from the bony bump at the base of your neck (C7 vertebra) to the top of your hip bones.
- Small: 15-17 inch torso
- Medium: 17-19 inch torso
- Large: 19-21 inch torso
