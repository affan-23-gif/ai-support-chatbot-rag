# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Pinecrest gift cards.

## Pay in installments
Orders over $150 can be split into 4 interest-free payments every 2 weeks with Afterpay at checkout. Approval is decided by Afterpay.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If part of the order ships later, you are charged for each part as it ships.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is charged on US orders based on the shipping address, as required by state law.
