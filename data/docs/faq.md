# Frequently Asked Questions

## Contact and support hours
Live chat with our team is available 8 am to 8 pm Eastern Time, Monday to Saturday. Email support@pinecrest-demo.example any time. When a conversation is passed to our team, a person replies by email within 1 business day.

## Trail Points loyalty program
Members earn 1 Trail Point for every $1 spent. 200 points can be redeemed for $10 off a future order. Points expire after 12 months without a purchase. Joining is free from your account page.

## Price match
We match the price of an identical item (same brand, model, colour and size) sold by a major US retailer within 14 days of your purchase. Marketplace sellers, clearance and coupon prices are not eligible. Contact us with a link to the lower price.

## Gift cards
Gift cards never expire and have no fees. They can be used online only and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, from your account page. After that the order may already be packed; contact us and we will try, or you can return it after delivery.

## Student and military discount
Students and US military members get 10% off full-price items after verifying with our partner ID check on the Discounts page. It cannot be combined with other discounts.
