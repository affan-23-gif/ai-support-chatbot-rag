# Pinecrest Warranty

## What is covered
Pinecrest-brand backpacks and tents have a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship, starting from the delivery date. Pinecrest-brand trekking poles have a lifetime warranty against breakage in normal use. Other brands are covered by their own manufacturer's warranty, and we help you file the claim.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or accidents, UV fading, damage from animals, and gear that has been modified or repaired by someone else.

## How to make a warranty claim
Email warranty@pinecrest-demo.example with your order number, a short description of the problem and 2-3 photos. Our team replies within 2 business days. If the claim is approved we repair the item, replace it, or give store credit if the item is no longer made. Repairs take 2-3 weeks plus shipping.
