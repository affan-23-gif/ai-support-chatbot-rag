# Shipping Policy

## Processing time
Orders are packed and shipped within 1-2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or US holidays start processing the next business day. You get a confirmation email with a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

## Shipping options in the US
- Standard shipping: 3-7 business days. Free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95.
- Expedited shipping: 2-3 business days for $14.95.
- Overnight shipping: next business day for $29.95. Order before 12 pm Eastern Time for same-day dispatch.

Overnight shipping is not available to PO boxes, Alaska or Hawaii. Standard shipping to Alaska and Hawaii takes 7-12 business days.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and all European Union countries. International orders take 7-14 business days and cost a flat $24.95. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery. We do not ship fuel canisters or bear spray outside the US.

## Lost or damaged packages
If tracking shows delivered but you can't find your package, wait 48 hours and check with neighbours, then contact us. We file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund once the claim is opened. Report damaged packages within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the box and the item.
