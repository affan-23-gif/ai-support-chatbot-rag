# Gear Care Guide

## Down jackets and sleeping bags
Wash in a front-loading machine on a gentle cold cycle with a down-specific soap. Tumble dry on low heat with 2-3 clean tennis balls to break up clumps; drying can take 2-3 hours. Never dry clean down.

## Waterproof rain shells
Wash with a technical-fabric cleaner, not regular detergent or fabric softener. When water stops beading on the surface, restore the water repellency with a spray-on DWR treatment and tumble dry on low for 20 minutes.

## Tents
Always dry your tent completely before storing it to prevent mildew. Store it loosely in a cool, dry place, not tightly packed in its stuff sack. Clean with a sponge and cool water; never machine wash a tent.

## Hiking boots
Remove the insoles and dry boots at room temperature, away from heaters or fires, which can crack leather and weaken glue. Treat leather boots with a conditioner 2-3 times a season.
